**ORDER \& OPERATIONS DASHBOARD**



**Project Overview**



This project presents an interactive Order \& Operations Dashboard built to analyze business performance across sales, operations, and logistics. The goal is to transform raw transactional data into actionable insights that support data-driven decision-making.



**Business Problem**

Organizations often struggle to:

* Track overall sales performance in real time
* Identify high-performing product categories
* Understand regional demand patterns
* Optimize shipping and delivery strategies

This dashboard solves these problems by providing a centralized view of key operational metrics.



**Key Insights Delivered**



1. 38K total quantity sold, indicating strong product movement
2. Nearly 10K orders processed, showing high transaction volume
3. Regional disparities in demand, with some regions outperforming others
4. Standard Class shipping dominates\*\*, highlighting cost-efficiency preference
5. Increasing order trends over time, suggesting business growth



**Dashboard Breakdown**



* Orders Over Time
1. Time-series analysis to track growth and seasonality
2. Helps identify peak sales periods and demand fluctuations



* Quantity \& Orders by Sub-Category
1. Compares product demand vs order frequency
2. Highlights top-performing categories like Binders and Paper



* Orders by Region

Visual distribution across:

* West
* East
* Central
* South

Enables geographic performance comparison



* Orders by Ship Mode

Shows logistics preferences:

1. Standard Class (most used)
2. Second Class
3. First Class
4. Same Day
