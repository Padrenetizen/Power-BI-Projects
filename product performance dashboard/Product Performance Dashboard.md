**Product Performance Dashboard**



**Overview**

The Product Performance Dashboard is a business intelligence project designed to analyze sales, revenue, and profitability across products and categories. It provides key insights into top-performing products, customer activity, and overall business performance.

This dashboard helps stakeholders quickly identify:

\* Best-selling products

\* Most profitable products

\* Sales trends by sub-category

\* Products with high sales but low profitability



**Objectives**



* Track overall business performance using key metrics
* Identify top-performing and underperforming products
* Analyze sales distribution across product categories
* Support data-driven decision-making



**Key Metrics**



* Total Profit: 286.4K
* Total Revenue: 2.30M
* Total Products: 1862
* Total Orders: 9994
* Total Customers: 793



**Dashboard Features**



* Top 10 Best-Selling Products: Displays products generating the highest sales volume, helping identify high-demand items.
* Top 10 Most Profitable Products: Highlights products contributing the most to profit, which may differ from best-selling items.
* Sales by Sub-Category: Breaks down revenue across different product sub-categories such as:
1. Phones
2. Chairs
3. Storage
4. Tables
5. Accessories

This helps uncover which categories drive the most revenue.

* Sales vs Profit Analysis: A scatter plot comparing:
1. Maximum Sales
2. Minimum Profit

Used to identify:

1. High-sales, low-profit products (potential inefficiencies)
2. Loss-making products



**Tools \& Technologies**

* Power BI (or specify if Tableau/Excel)
* Data modeling \& visualization
* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)



**Dataset**

The dashboard is built using the Superstore dataset, which includes:

* Orders
* Products
* Customers
* Sales \& Profit data



**Insights**



1. Some products generate high sales but low or negative profit
2. A few products dominate both sales and profitability
3. Sub-categories like Phones and Chairs contribute the most revenue
4. Low-performing categories may need strategic review.
