**Superstore Delivery Analysis Dashboard**



**Overview**



The superstore Delivery Analysis Dashboard provides insights into delivery performance, shipping efficiency, customer activity, and regional order distribution using the Sample Superstore dataset.



The goal of this dashboard is to analyze how quickly orders are delivered, identify the most efficient shipping modes, and understand how delivery performance varies across regions, months, and product categories.



This analysis helps businesses improve logistics operations, optimize shipping strategies, and enhance customer satisfaction.



---



**Business Questions Answered**



This dashboard was designed to answer the following key business questions:



\* How many days does it take on average for customers to receive their packages?

\* Which shipping mode delivers products the fastest?

\* How does delivery time change across different months?

\* Which regions generate the highest number of orders?

\* Which shipping modes generate the most revenue?

\* Which product categories experience longer delivery times?

\* Is there any relationship between delivery days and sales performance?



---



**Key Metrics**



The dashboard highlights several important KPIs:



\* Total Customers: 5,009

\* Total Transactions: 9,994

\* Fastest Shipping Mode: Standard Class

\* Median Delivery Days: 4 Days

\* Total Revenue:\*\* 1.49K



These metrics provide a quick overview of delivery performance and customer activity.



---



**Dashboard Features**



1\. Average Delivery Days by Month



A line chart showing how delivery times fluctuate throughout the year.

This helps identify seasonal patterns or potential operational delays.



2\. Orders by Region



A bar chart displaying the distribution of orders across regions (West, East, Central, South).

This helps identify where delivery demand is highest.



3\. Total Sales by Ship Mode



A horizontal bar chart comparing revenue generated by different shipping methods.

This helps determine which shipping options customers prefer.



4\. Delivery Days vs Sales by Product



A scatter plot that explores the relationship between delivery time and product sales.



5\. Delivery Time by Product Category



A stacked bar chart showing how long it takes for customers to receive products across different product types.



---



**Filters**



The dashboard includes interactive filters that allow users to explore the data:



\* Year Filter: Analyze delivery performance by year (2018–2021)

\* Month Filter: Drill down into monthly delivery trends

Key Insights



Some insights from the analysis include:



\* The West region has the highest order volume, indicating a high logistics demand in that area.

\* Standard Class shipping generates the highest sales, suggesting it is the most commonly used shipping method.

\* The average delivery time remains around 4 days, indicating relatively stable delivery performance throughout the year.

\* Certain product categories experience longer delivery times, which may indicate logistical challenges.
