**Superstore Purchase Analysis Dashboard**



**Overview**



The Superstore Purchase Analysis Dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of sales performance, profitability, product demand, and customer purchasing trends using the Sample Superstore dataset.



This dashboard helps stakeholders monitor revenue growth, identify high-performing product categories, and understand purchasing behavior across different time periods. The analysis supports data-driven decision-making by highlighting key financial and operational metrics.



---



**Business Questions Answered**



This dashboard was designed to answer important business questions such as:



\* What is the total revenue generated by the business?

\* How profitable are the company’s operations?

\* Which product sub-categories generate the most sales?

\* Which product categories contribute the most profit?

\* How does revenue change across different months?

\* Which shipping modes are most commonly used by customers?



---



**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**



The dashboard highlights several important metrics:



\* Total Revenue: 2.3M

\* Median Profit Margin: 2.86

\* Total Sales: 149K

\* Total Transactions: 9,994

\* Total Profit: 286.4K



These KPIs provide a quick snapshot of overall business performance.



---



**Dashboard Features**



**Revenue Trend Analysis**



A monthly revenue line chart shows how sales change throughout the year, helping identify seasonal patterns and growth periods.



**Product Sales Performance**



The Sales by Sub-Category bar chart highlights which products generate the most revenue, helping businesses identify their top-selling items.



**Profit Contribution by Category**



A donut chart visualizes profit contribution across the three major product categories:



\* Technology

\* Office Supplies

\* Furniture



**Shipping Mode Analysis**



The Orders by Ship Mode visualization shows how customers prefer their orders to be delivered, helping businesses optimize logistics strategies.



---



**Interactive Filters**



The dashboard includes slicers that allow users to explore the data dynamically:



\* Year Filter: Analyze performance across multiple years (2018–2021)

\* Month Filter: Drill down into specific monthly trends



These filters allow users to customize the dashboard view and perform deeper analysis.



---



**Key Insights**



Some key insights from the analysis include:



\* Technology products generate the highest profit, making them the most profitable category.

\* Phones and Chairs are the top-selling sub-categories, contributing significantly to total revenue.

\* Revenue increases toward the end of the year, indicating strong seasonal demand.

\* Standard Class shipping is the most commonly used shipping method, suggesting customers prefer cost-efficient delivery options.
