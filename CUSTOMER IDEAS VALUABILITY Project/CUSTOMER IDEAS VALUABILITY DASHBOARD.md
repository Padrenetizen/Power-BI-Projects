**Recommendations**

1. Improve Idea Implementation Rates: Establish a structured idea evaluation and implementation process to convert high-value customer and employee suggestions into actionable projects. Prioritize ideas based on ROI, business value, feasibility, and implementation costs.
2. Prioritize High-ROI Innovation Categories: Increase investment in idea categories generating the highest ROI multipliers and innovation value. Allocate resources strategically to Product Improvement, Service Innovation, Technology Upgrade, and Process Optimization based on their respective industry performance.
3. Strengthen Customer-Centric Innovation: Introduce regular customer feedback programmes, surveys, and engagement initiatives to identify unmet needs and encourage valuable idea submissions. Pay particular attention to high-contributing customer tiers while ensuring ideas from all customer segments are considered.
