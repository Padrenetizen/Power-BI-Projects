**PROJECT TITLE:** CUSTOMER LOYALTY DRIVERS DASHBOARD



**PROJECT OVERVIEW**: This project analyzes the rate of customer loyalty by industry, and year.



**BUSINESS PROBLEM**: Why are high-value customers becoming dissatisfied?



**DATASET**: Innovation and customer experience dataset.



**DATA MODEL**: None



**TOOLS \& TECHNOLOGIES**: PowerBI



**KPIS**:

* Total customer loyalty index
* Median satisfaction score
* Customer Loyalty Index

**FILTERS**

* Industry
* Interaction Year

**RECOMMENDATIONS**

* Improve Customer Retention and Loyalty: Retention rates indicate opportunities to strengthen long-term customer relationships across manufacturing, retail, and technology. Analyze churn patterns, customer satisfaction, and loyalty indicators to identify at-risk customers and implement targeted retention programs, personalized offers, and proactive follow-ups.



* Optimize Customer Interaction Channels: Evaluate customer loyalty and satisfaction across Phone, Chat, Email, Help Desk, and Social Media. Identify the channels with weaker performance and improve response times, service quality, and issue resolution to deliver a consistent customer experience across all industries.



* Develop Customer-Tier-Specific Strategies: Compare engagement, satisfaction, and loyalty across Basic, Silver, Gold, and Platinum customers. Identify the practices associated with stronger performance in each industry and use these insights to create targeted engagement and loyalty initiatives that improve outcomes across all customer tiers.
