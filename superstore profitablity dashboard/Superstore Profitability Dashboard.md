**Superstore Profitability Dashboard**



**Overview**



The Superstore Profitability Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence dashboard built in Power BI using the Sample Superstore dataset. It provides insights into profitability, customer activity, product performance, and regional profit distribution to support data-driven decision making. The dashboard allows users to quickly analyze which segments, categories, and locations generate the most profit, while also identifying underperforming products.



**Objectives**



The main goals of this dashboard are to:

* Monitor overall company profitability
* Identify top and lowest performing products
* Analyze profit distribution across customer segments
* Understand profit contribution by product category
* Evaluate profit margin performance across states
* Provide interactive filtering by year and month



**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**



The dashboard highlights several core business metrics:

1. Total Profit: Overall profit generated from all orders
2. Total Products: Number of unique products sold
3. Total Profit Margin: Overall profitability ratio
4. Total Customers: Unique customers in the dataset
5. Total Orders: Total number of transactions



**Dashboard Insights**



* Product Performance

The dashboard highlights products generating the lowest sales, helping businesses identify underperforming items that may require pricing adjustments, marketing improvements, or discontinuation.



* Profit by Segment

Profit is broken down across three customer segments:

\* Consumer

\* Corporate

\* Home Office

This helps businesses understand which customer group drives the most profitability.



* Profit by Category

Profit distribution is analyzed across the three product categories:

\* Technology

\* Office Supplies

\* Furniture

This enables stakeholders to identify which product category contributes the most to overall profit.



* Profit Margin by State

A regional analysis of profitability across states helps businesses:

\* Identify high-performing markets

\* Detect regions with low profitability

\* Support regional sales strategy planning



**Filters**

The dashboard includes interactive filters that allow users to explore the data dynamically:

\* Year Filter: 2018 – 2021

\* Month Filter: January – December

These filters enable time-based analysis of profit performance.



**Tools \& Technologies**

\* Power BI – Data modeling and visualization

\* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – KPI calculations and measures

\* Sample Superstore Dataset – Retail sales dataset used for analysis



**Business Value**

This dashboard helps decision-makers:

\* Track financial performance quickly

\* Identify profitable customer segments

\* Detect underperforming products

\* Understand geographic profit distribution

\* Improve inventory and marketing strategies
